One place to report a bug

In one lineThe in-app report, the support inbox and the chat form become one report, filed straight into the tracker. From spec 0012

One report, Ana's crash on export, from her click to the tracker

Now
Ana hits the crash on export
↓ three ways in, none of them chosen
in-app report · support inbox · chat form
↓ someone copies it by hand
a tracker issue, written a day later, missing the version

3 places a report can land, 1 copy by hand, and 0 of them carry the app version.

After
Ana hits the crash on export
↓ one way in
in-app report, with the version and the last screen attached
↓ filed as she sends it
a tracker issue, the same minute, with the version

1 place a report lands, 0 copies by hand, and every report carries the app version.
