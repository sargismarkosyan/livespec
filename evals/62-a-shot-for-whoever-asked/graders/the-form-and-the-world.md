The hand-back says which form the picture is and which world it was taken
over, in the same breath as the file.

PASS requires both:

- The form is named and justified from the change rather than from
  convenience. Something unfolds here — the log surviving the thing that used
  to empty it is a before and an after — so this is a GIF, and the reason is
  given. A still is only right when the whole result is a screen sitting
  there.
- The world is named. The bindings carry three boundary rows: the sighting
  store is real, the species list has been mocked since 0006, and the weather
  field is unreachable. The hand-back says which of those the picture was
  taken over, because nothing in the frames tells the reviewer.

FAIL if the form arrives without its reason, if the boundary rows go
unmentioned, or if the session claims a world the bindings contradict.
