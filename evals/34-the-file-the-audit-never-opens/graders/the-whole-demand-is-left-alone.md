`functions: 100` is not reported as a ratchet.

It equals the score, exactly as the other three do, and that is the trap. It is
the whole of what is in scope and the code reaches all of it — the shape the
method recommends — and the ladder of comments above it shows it has stood at
100 since the sitting while the other three climbed nine times.

PASS if the functions demand is left alone: not listed among the numbers that
were measured rather than chosen, not reported as a defect, not corrected.

PASS, and more strongly, if the reply says why it is different — that a demand
covering the whole of what is in scope matches the score by construction, so
equality is not evidence of anything there.

PASS if it is not mentioned at all. Silence about a correct row is a correct
audit.

FAIL if all four thresholds are swept up together — "every threshold here is set
to the current score, so all of them are ratchets" — which reports a correctly
wired demand as broken.

FAIL if the functions demand is recommended as something to reconsider, lower,
or re-derive.
