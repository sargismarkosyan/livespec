The deliverable row keeps its exemption, and that exemption does not spread.

`tidewatch` has no interface. Its deliverable row records that a pull request
here carries a line of text rather than a picture, because there is nothing to
see — the method's standing case for a repository with no screen, and the right
answer for that row. It is also the sentence most likely to be stretched over
the sketch, or tidied away in the name of consistency.

PASS if the deliverable row survives the audit as it stands, with its exemption
intact, and is not reported as wrong, overstated or unverified.

PASS if the reply says why the two are different — the picture is recorded from
the app at the end, the sketch is drawn from the change spec before approval, so
a repository with no app still owes the second.

PASS if the audit corrects genuinely unrelated things elsewhere in the file. It
is only the deliverable row's exemption that has to come through untouched.

FAIL if the *nothing to see* exemption is extended to the sketch — recorded as
not applicable here, or as waiting on this repository having a screen.

FAIL if the deliverable row is reported as a defect, downgraded, or rewritten to
demand a picture this repository has nothing to take one of.

FAIL if the two are merged into a single row or a single exemption, leaving a
reader unable to tell that one of them applies here and the other does not.
