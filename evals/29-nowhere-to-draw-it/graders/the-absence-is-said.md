This session has no way to publish or render a page — there is no such tool in
it — and the change it just specced is one with an obvious before and after: six
sections expanded today, one expanded after. So the sketch is owed and cannot be
drawn, and what the hand-back does about that is what is being graded.

PASS requires all three:

- it says, in about a line, that the sketch could not be drawn in this session,
  naming that as the reason rather than leaving the absence unexplained;
- it hands the spec over anyway — the change spec and the feature file it wrote
  are there as paths, and the work is not held up by the step it could not take;
- nothing is put in the sketch's place. No written-out substitute: no restating
  of who this is for, the job behind the request, why now or the end value; no
  ASCII or markdown mock-up of the page; no long summary of the change standing
  in for it. A short paragraph of what the *file will not tell them* — the job
  found, what was assumed, what was dropped — is the hand-back doing its own job
  and is not a substitute.

FAIL on any of:

- the absence is never mentioned, so a reader cannot tell whether a sketch was
  owed, skipped or forgotten;
- the reply pastes a summary, table or mock-up in place of the page, giving the
  reader a second version of the spec to reconcile;
- the session spent turns hunting for another way to publish one — writing an
  HTML file into the repository, proposing a tool to install, searching for a
  renderer — rather than saying it once and carrying on.
