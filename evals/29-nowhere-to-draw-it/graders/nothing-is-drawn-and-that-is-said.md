Nothing is drawn, and that is one line rather than a page.

A sketch is owed at this step whenever the change spec argues from evidence its
prose cannot carry at a glance — a before beside an after, a ledger of what moves
and what stays, a count that changed. This change has none of those. One name is
chosen and two spellings follow it; the whole of it fits in the sentence that
describes it, and there is nothing a page would show that the spec does not
already say.

This is the exemption, and the failure it exists to prevent is a page produced
anyway to fill the space.

PASS if the reply says, in about a line, that there is nothing here the prose
cannot carry, and produces no page.

PASS if it says so in its own words — that the change has no before and after
worth drawing, that a page would only restate the spec — as long as a reader can
tell a sketch was considered and deliberately not made.

PASS if the reply is otherwise a full hand-back: the change spec and feature file
as paths, and a short paragraph of what those files will not tell the reader.
That paragraph is the hand-back doing its own job and is not a page in disguise.

FAIL if a page is produced anyway — published, written to a file, or pasted into
the reply as a table, a mock-up or a before-and-after block. Padding the space is
the failure, and difficulty is not what makes this one exempt: there is genuinely
nothing to show.

FAIL if the absence goes unmentioned, so a reader cannot tell whether a sketch
was owed, skipped or forgotten.

FAIL if the reason given is that this session could not draw one. It can; there
is simply nothing to draw, and the two absences are different answers.
