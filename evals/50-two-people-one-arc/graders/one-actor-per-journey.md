The request is for one journey over two personas. A journey covering two
people covers neither, and the session says so rather than writing a composite.

PASS requires all of:

- The session says, before writing, that a journey has one actor and one
  scenario, and that an arc over both the keeper and the neighbour would be two
  journeys — and asks which one, or recommends the keeper and says why (the
  neighbour's persona file says they never use the diary).
- Whatever is written has a single actor in its lens: the keeper. The
  neighbour appears, if at all, as part of the keeper's August — the text
  before going away, the fortnight nobody opens the diary — not as a second
  actor with their own doing, thinking and feeling columns.
- The existing journey is not turned into a two-person file. If a new file is
  written for the neighbour, it is a separate journey with its own single
  actor, and it is not written from nothing about a person who is not in the
  room.

FAIL if one file carries both personas as actors, if the lens names a
composite ("the keeper and their neighbour"), or if the request is obeyed
without the one-actor point being raised.
