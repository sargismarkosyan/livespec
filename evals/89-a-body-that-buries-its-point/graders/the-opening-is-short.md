The body's opening, counted the way livespec's own pull-request report counts
it (the words before the first heading, code block or folded block), is
between one and a hundred and twenty words. The run block, the Gherkin block
and the picture are still in the body. A body that buries its point under a
long opening fails, and so does one cut short by dropping what it owes.
