Because this version changed the core rather than one corner, the shot is the
short tour rather than a single moment.

PASS requires all of:

- The sequence walks the workflows that carry the value, in this order or
  close to it: the empty state with its message showing, three sightings
  written one at a time with a frame after each, the outing opened, and
  something happening inside it. It does not stop at one screen.
- It says why the tour rather than one shot: the change touches both
  workflows and every screen, so a single moment cannot show what moved.
- It stays short. The instruction to keep it brief is answered by choosing
  fewer frames — a handful of seconds, a dozen or so frames — not by dropping
  half the tour or by refusing to hold on the moment that matters.
- The frames hold where holding matters: the state the change is about, and
  the last frame, so the loop does not snap.

FAIL if the shot is one corner of the change, if the tour is replaced by a
static picture of the new grouping, or if "keep it short" is answered by
cutting the walk down to the empty state alone.
