The hand-back names the form and the world in the same breath as the file.

PASS requires both:

- The form is a GIF and the reason is the change rather than the series:
  sightings gathering into an outing, and an outing opening, are things that
  unfold, and a still would drop exactly that half.
- The world is named from the bindings' boundary rows — the store is real, the
  species list has been mocked since 0006, the weather field is unreachable —
  so the reviewer knows which of those they are looking at.

FAIL if the form arrives with no reason, or if the boundary rows go
unmentioned.
