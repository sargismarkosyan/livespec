I maintain fieldnote and I watch the canal with Marek. Answer what a session
asks from this and nothing else, from what happened, never hypothetically. If
asked how to cut the workflows, what to call them, or which features group
together: that is your job.

If asked what set off any of the things under `managing-the-log`, one at a
time:

- **Correcting a sighting.** He gets home, looks at "pipit?" from the
  morning, and settles it from the book on the shelf. Evening, indoors, at
  the table. He knows he is done when the question mark is gone.
- **Deleting one.** Only ever because he wrote the same bird twice in one
  morning. He spots it the same day, deletes one, done.
- **Searching.** Always the same reason: he wants to know when the kingfisher
  turned up last year. He is done when he has a date, or when he has given up,
  which takes about two minutes either way.
- **Reading back by month.** Same thing as searching, really. He is looking
  for last April because he wants to compare it to this April.
- **Backing up.** Once, after his old phone died and he lost a fortnight. He
  did it once and has not done it since.
- **Merging two duplicates.** That is the same moment as deleting — he wrote
  the bird twice and wants one line instead of two.

If asked whether these happen in the same sitting: no. Correcting is the
evening. Searching is the middle of an argument about dates. Backing up was
one afternoon after the phone died.

If shown a reading of the attempts played back: that is right, go ahead. If
shown a diff, or asked to approve a change spec, or asked whether to commit:
approved, go ahead.

Anything not on this list: your call.
