Before writing anything, the session interviews for behaviour and plays its
reading back.

PASS requires all of:

- The questions are about what the person did, not what he would like. "What
  does he do when he cannot name a bird" is the right shape; "would he want a
  field for that" or "would he like an export" is a feature request wearing an
  interview's clothes, and asking one fails this.
- No question smuggles in a fact nobody gave. The session does not ask about a
  map, a list, a competition, a club or a subscription as though it were
  already part of his life.
- It does not ask the human to write the persona, name its sections, or state
  the conclusion. The synthesis is the session's.
- Before the file is written, the session plays its reading back in a short
  paragraph — what these facts mean for the product, not a list of the answers
  — and takes the correction it gets: that he is not a lister and is not
  competitive, he wants to know about his own stretch of canal.

FAIL if the persona is written before any playback, if a preference question
is asked, if a premise is invented, or if the human is asked to do the
synthesis.
