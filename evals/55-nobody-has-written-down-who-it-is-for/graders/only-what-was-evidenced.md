The file says only what somebody actually gave the session, and puts the rest
where the next reader will ask rather than assume.

PASS requires all of:

- The one refusal in it is the evidenced one: the county recorder asked twice
  and he said no twice. Nothing he was never asked about — paying for it,
  wanting a map, an export, an identification guide, reminders — appears as a
  refusal or as a preference, because the only answer given about any of them
  was that nobody has asked him.
- What nobody knows is at the foot as an open question rather than resolved
  by inference. The unsettled bird with a question mark after it, or whether
  he would pay, are the candidates; at least one open question is there.
- No provenance in the file. Who was asked, when, and how much was inferred
  belong in the change spec, and the change spec says it.

FAIL if a preference nobody stated is written as fact, if the open questions
section is empty or missing while the sitting plainly left things unknown, or
if the file opens with how it was made.
