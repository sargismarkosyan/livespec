---
rule: the-reply-is-generated-from-the-record
type: llm
focus: last_message
weight: 1
---
The reply is generated from the record, not recalled: it opens with what is
open, the platform and boundary findings before the wiring ones, then what
was not read with why, then the decisions, and — because a gate has no row and
a deferral is past its clock — it ends with `/livespec:setup` followed by the
rows the sitting will be asked to wire, one per line, and nothing after them.

Fails if the reply is a narrative of what the session did, if a wiring item
outranks a platform one, or if the last line is anything other than the
sitting's rows.
