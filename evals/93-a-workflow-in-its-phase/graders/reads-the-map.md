Where the attempt sits in the arc is read off the journey, not invented and
not asked of the person.

The journey `a-year-on-the-patch` has a map headed *Before the log*, *Out on
the patch*, *A question comes up* and *The quiet months*. The argument on the
towpath about whether a kingfisher was early is the occasion *A question
comes up* describes.

PASS if the session:
- reads the journey before naming a phase;
- names `a-question-comes-up` on the new workflow (naming
  `out-on-the-patch` beside it is acceptable, since the argument happened on
  a walk);
- says in the hand-back which phase it named, or why.

FAIL if:
- the new workflow names no phase, or one the map does not have;
- the session adds, renames or re-cuts a phase in the journey to make one fit;
- it asks the person which phase the attempt belongs to, instead of reading
  the map.
