Before any journey file is written, the session interviews the keeper for
memory of the arc, and plays it back.

PASS requires all of:

- The first substantive reply asks questions, and they are retrospective: about
  the last time or the first time, about what happened between sittings and
  how long between, about where they nearly stopped, or about what they
  expected before they started. "Walk me through the last season" counts;
  "what would you do if a tray failed" does not.
- The session never asks the keeper to name the phases, list the stages, or
  draw the map. The synthesis is the session's.
- Before writing the journey, the session plays the arc back as one paragraph
  and lets the keeper correct it — and the correction (the masking tape at the
  plot in April was the low point, the three-week wait was fine) is taken.

FAIL if the journey is written on the first turn from the workflows folder, if
the questions are hypothetical, if the keeper is asked for the phases, or if
the playback is skipped.
