Who was asked, when, and how much was inferred belongs in the change spec
under `specs/changes/`, and not in the journey.

PASS requires both:

- A numbered change spec was written, and it says where the arc came from:
  the keeper's own seasons, told in this sitting — lived rather than
  predicted. "Drawn from the keeper's first and most recent seasons" is
  enough. It is better still if it says what the session inferred (the
  phase cuts, the curve), but that is not required for a pass.
- The journey file itself carries no provenance: no "written from an interview
  with Rowan", no date of the sitting, no note on how it was made. The one
  thing it may carry at its foot is the question nobody answered — whether the
  summer silence is the diary having done its job or having been given up on.

FAIL if the journey opens or closes with how it was produced, or if no change
spec says where the arc came from.
