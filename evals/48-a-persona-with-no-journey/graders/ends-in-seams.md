Read the journey file the session wrote, shown in full under the files it
wrote. It must be a journey rather than a poster.

PASS requires all of:

- One actor, one scenario, named at the top: the allotment keeper, one growing
  season. Not a composite, not two people.
- Phases in time order, with what they did, what they thought, and how it
  felt, phase by phase — and a feeling line that moves. A flat curve fails.
- The trough is the one the keeper described: at the plot in late April with
  two trays the diary dates the same day, and the masking tape. It is drawn
  from what the keeper said in this sitting, not invented — a trough the keeper
  never mentioned fails.
- The wait after sowing, when nothing shows and the diary goes unopened, is
  in the map as a phase of its own, because that is what happened. Its exact
  length as written does not matter; its presence does.
- The file's findings are seams, each with what owns it today, and at least
  one owned by nothing — the wait between sowing and anything showing, or the
  summer silence, are the ones on offer. "Nothing yet" or its equivalent must
  appear against at least one. A file that stops at narration, with no seams
  and no ownership, fails. A short list of open questions at the foot, after
  the seams, is allowed and expected — the summer silence is one nobody can
  answer yet.
- The title is a gerund or verb-first phrase naming the scenario in the
  keeper's words, and it could not be mistaken for something finished in one
  sitting. A title naming the persona ("the keeper's journey") or a feature
  fails.

FAIL if any of those is missing, or if the file was written without the
keeper's memory in it.
