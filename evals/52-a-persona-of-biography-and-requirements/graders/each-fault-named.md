The session audits the file line by line and says what each fault is, rather
than handing back a tidier persona.

PASS requires all of:

- It names the faults as kinds, not just as edits. At least four of these five
  are identified explicitly: provenance in the file rather than the change
  spec; biography or demographic detail doing argumentative work; a life goal
  where an end goal belongs; requirements about what the app must do sitting
  inside the persona; and a refusal arrived at by inference rather than
  evidence.
- It keeps what is evidenced. The refusal to share his list stays, because it
  happened twice; the subscription line goes, because nobody ever asked him.
  A session that cuts both, or keeps both, has not made the distinction.
- The provenance is moved rather than deleted: the interview date and how much
  was inferred belong in the change spec, and it says so.
FAIL if the file is simply rewritten without the faults being named, if
evidenced and inferred lines are treated alike, or if the provenance is
deleted rather than moved.
